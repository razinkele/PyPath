COMPREHENSIVE TECHNICAL & ECOLOGICAL REVIEW
Curonian Lagoon Ecopath with Ecosim Case Study Model
35-Group Multistanza Model (PEST-Calibrated)
Review Date: 2026-10-23

================================================================================
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
================================================================================

The Curonian Lagoon 35-group multistanza Ecopath/Ecosim model represents a 
technically competent application of the PyPath framework but exhibits CRITICAL 
ECOLOGICAL CONCERNS that warrant archival from public release.

OVERALL ASSESSMENT: PRELIMINARY - NOT READY FOR PUBLIC USE

Key Concerns (Ranked by Severity):
1. [CRITICAL] Perfect convergence (SS=0.000000) suggests overfitting to limited 
   fishery data; ecological implausibility of zero residual error
2. [HIGH] Inadequate ecosystem-wide validation; calibration uses only landing 
   statistics, ignoring benthic biomass, zooplankton composition, predation 
   patterns
3. [HIGH] Questionable inclusion of Acartia (stenohaline marine) in freshwater/
   brackish system; zooplankton composition unvalidated
4. [MEDIUM] Water transparency, habitat zoning, food web connections not 
   empirically verified
5. [MEDIUM] Unrealistic stability across fishing pressure suggests under-
   parameterized predator-prey dynamics

RECOMMENDATION: Keep archived until field validation completed.
