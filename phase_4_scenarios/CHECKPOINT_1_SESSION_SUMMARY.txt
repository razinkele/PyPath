FIVE SCENARIOS DEFINED
================================================================================

1. BASELINE (BAU) - Business-as-Usual
   Code: BAU
   Fishing Multiplier: 1.0× all species
   Expected Catch: 12-15 tonnes/year
   Expected Biomass: 2,100-2,400 tonnes
   Economic Value: €65,000-95,000/year
   Ecosystem Stability: 0.25 (moderate)
   Policy Focus: Status quo reference scenario

2. FISHING REDUCTION (RED50) - Conservation Focus
   Code: RED50
   Fishing Multiplier: 0.5× all species
   Expected Catch: 6-8 tonnes/year
   Expected Biomass: 2,900-3,200 tonnes (+35-40% vs. baseline)
   Economic Value: €35,000-55,000/year
   Ecosystem Stability: 0.20 (excellent - best)
   Policy Focus: Stock rebuilding and ecosystem recovery

3. FISHING INCREASE (INC30) - Productivity Focus
   Code: INC30
   Fishing Multiplier: 1.3× all species
   Expected Catch: 16-20 tonnes/year (years 1-3 only)
   Expected Biomass: 1,600-1,900 tonnes (-25-35% vs. baseline)
   Economic Value: €95,000-140,000/year (declining after year 3)
   Ecosystem Stability: 0.35-0.45 (poor - worst)
   Policy Focus: Short-term yield maximization
   WARNING: Unsustainable; boom-bust cycle by year 8-11

4. SPATIAL CLOSURE (MPA25) - Marine Protected Area
   Code: MPA25
   Fishing Multiplier: 0.75× all species (75% accessible area)
   Protected Area: 25% of lagoon (northern sanctuary)
   Expected Catch: 10-13 tonnes/year
   Expected Biomass: 2,300-2,600 tonnes (+9-25% vs. baseline)
   Economic Value: €60,000-85,000/year
   Ecosystem Stability: 0.22-0.24 (good)
   Policy Focus: Ecosystem protection with spillover benefits
   Spillover Effect: +15-25% juvenile recruitment by years 6-11

5. SELECTIVE SPECIES (SEL) - Economic Optimization
   Code: SEL
   Fishing Multiplier: Bream 1.2×, Perch 0.7×, Roach 0.6×, Vimba 0.8×, Zander 1.3×
   Expected Catch: 10-13 tonnes/year
   Expected Biomass: 2,200-2,500 tonnes (+5-20% vs. baseline)
   Economic Value: €90,000-140,000/year (+35-45% sustainable)
   Value Per Tonne: €8-12/kg (vs. €5-8/kg baseline)
   Ecosystem Stability: 0.24-0.26 (good)
   Policy Focus: Maximize economic value through selective harvesting
