FIVE SCENARIOS DEFINED FOR 2025-2035 MODELING
================================================================================

1. BASELINE (BAU) - Status Quo
   Code: BAU
   Fishing Multiplier: 1.0x all species
   Expected Annual Catch: 12-15 tonnes
   Expected Mean Biomass: 2,100-2,400 tonnes
   Ecosystem Stability: 0.25 (moderate)
   Economic Value: €65,000-95,000/year
   Policy Goal: Maintain current fishing pressure (reference scenario)

2. FISHING REDUCTION (RED50) - Conservation Focus
   Code: RED50
   Fishing Multiplier: 0.5x all species (50% effort reduction)
   Expected Annual Catch: 6-8 tonnes
   Expected Mean Biomass: 2,900-3,200 tonnes (+35-40%)
   Ecosystem Stability: 0.20 (excellent)
   Economic Value: €35,000-55,000/year
   Policy Goal: Stock rebuilding through ecosystem-based management

3. FISHING INCREASE (INC30) - Productivity Focus
   Code: INC30
   Fishing Multiplier: 1.3x all species (30% effort increase)
   Expected Annual Catch: 16-20 tonnes (years 1-3 only; declines after)
   Expected Mean Biomass: 1,600-1,900 tonnes (-25-35%)
   Ecosystem Stability: 0.35-0.45 (poor)
   Economic Value: €95,000-140,000/year (short-term); declining to negative
   Policy Goal: Maximize short-term economic yield
   WARNING: Unsustainable; boom-bust cycle by year 8-11

4. SPATIAL CLOSURE (MPA25) - Protection with Spillover
   Code: MPA25
   Fishing Multiplier: 0.75x all species (proportional to 75% accessible area)
   Protected Area: 25% of lagoon (northern sanctuary, ~212 km²)
   Expected Annual Catch: 10-13 tonnes (-15-20%)
   Expected Mean Biomass: 2,300-2,600 tonnes (+9-25%)
   Ecosystem Stability: 0.22-0.24 (good)
   Economic Value: €60,000-85,000/year
   Policy Goal: Ecosystem protection with spillover benefits
   Spillover Effect: +15-25% juvenile recruitment by year 6-11

5. SELECTIVE SPECIES (SEL) - Economic Optimization
   Code: SEL
   Fishing Multiplier: Bream 1.2x, Perch 0.7x, Roach 0.6x, Vimba 0.8x, Zander 1.3x
   Seasonal Restriction: Zander March-April (spawning protection)
   Expected Annual Catch: 10-13 tonnes
   Expected Mean Biomass: 2,200-2,500 tonnes (+5-20%)
   Ecosystem Stability: 0.24-0.26 (good)
   Economic Value: €90,000-140,000/year (+35-45% sustainable)
   Policy Goal: Maximize economic value per tonne through selective harvesting
   Value Per Tonne: €8-12/kg (vs. €5-8/kg baseline)
