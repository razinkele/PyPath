ECOSYSTEM EFFECTS & DYNAMICS
================================================================================

Harvest Selectivity:
  - TARGETS: Large, older, premium-value individuals
  - PRESERVES: Smaller fish (recruitment reservoir)
  - PROTECTS: Vulnerable species (Vimba, young Perch)
  - EFFECT: Population age structure maintained, reproduction supported
  
Food Web Implications:
  - Predator feeding: SUPPORTED (smaller forage fish preserved)
  - Recruitment: ENHANCED (young fish escape selective harvest)
  - Trophic balance: IMPROVED (ecosystem function maintained)
  - Cascading effects: MINIMIZED (selective rather than guild-wide)
  
Population Dynamics:
  - Size structure: BIASED toward older individuals (selective removal)
  - Age structure: MORE BALANCED (young fish protected)
  - Sex ratios: LESS SKEWED (size-selective, not sex-specific)
  - Reproductive capacity: MAINTAINED (breeding population intact)
  
Resilience Effects:
  - Stock resilience: IMPROVED (diverse age structure)
  - Recruitment buffering: STRONG (young fish abundant)
  - Predator-prey oscillations: DAMPENED (stable predator food supply)
  - Environmental sensitivity: REDUCED (population redundancy)
