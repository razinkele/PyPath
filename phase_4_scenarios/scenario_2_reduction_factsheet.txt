POLICY IMPLICATIONS
================================================================================

STRENGTHS (+):
  [+] Enables ecosystem recovery (biomass stabilization)
  [+] Reduces overfishing-related collapses
  [+] Improves climate resilience (less stressed populations)
  [+] Aligns with EU Marine Strategy Framework Directive
  [+] Creates premium catch: fewer but larger fish
  [+] Long-term economic sustainability (avoids stock collapse)
  [+] Supports biodiversity and vulnerable species protection
  [+] Enables 50% recovery by 2035 (if maintained)

WEAKNESSES (-):
  [-] SEVERE SOCIOECONOMIC IMPACT: 50% immediate catch reduction
  [-] Job losses: ~50% fishing fleet employment reduction
  [-] Income loss: €500k-€1M annually (estimated)
  [-] Community resistance: Difficult to implement politically
  [-] Transition challenges: Retraining/alternative livelihoods needed
  [-] 2-3 year adjustment period required for markets
  [-] May drive illegal fishing (IUU)
  [-] Alternative income opportunities limited in region
