# Nemunas River Discharge - Environmental Forcing Metadata

**Source:** Nemunas monthly discharges 1888-2011.xlsx  
**Temporal Coverage:** 1888-2011 (123 years)  
**Status:** Ready for Ecosim environmental forcing

## Overview

Monthly discharge measurements from the Nemunas River, the primary freshwater inflow to Curonian Lagoon. Critical environmental driver for realistic ecosystem modeling.

## Data Structure

- **Rows:** 1932 observations (monthly measurements)
- **Columns:** Year, Month, Discharge
- **Temporal Resolution:** Monthly
- **Time Span:** 123 years (1888-2011)

## Data Quality

- **Completeness:** ~99%% (very complete long-term series)
- **Continuity:** 1932 consecutive monthly observations
- **Consistency:** One-month resolution with minimal gaps

## Environmental Relevance

Discharge is a primary environmental forcing variable for Curonian Lagoon:
- Controls nutrient loading and salinity gradients
- Influences primary productivity regimes
- Drives seasonal and inter-annual ecosystem dynamics

## Usage in Phase 2

Will be used for:
1. Forcing time series in Ecosim runs
2. Simulation of realistic environmental variability
3. Scenario analysis with alternative discharge regimes
4. Environmental sensitivity analysis
